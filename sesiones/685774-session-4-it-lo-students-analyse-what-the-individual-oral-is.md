📋 What is the IO anyway?

Now that you've felt the pressure of thinking on your feet, here is the actual structure of the IB English B HL Oral. It’s divided into three distinct "Acts":

  |  

Phase

   |  

Timing

   |  

What happens?

  |  

Preparation

   |  

20

Minutes

   |  

You get a literary extract (from a book we read) and a photo. You pick one and outline your thoughts.

  |  

Part 1: Presentation

   |  

3–4 Minutes

   |  

You speak uninterrupted about your chosen extract 

  |  

Part 2: Discussion

   |  

4–5 Minutes

   |  

We have a conversation about your presentation and the wider theme.

  |  

Part 3: General Chat

   |  

5–6 Minutes

   |  

We talk about at least one other prescribed theme from the course.

 Pro-Tips for Success

- It’s not a speech: It’s a structured conversation. If you stumble, just keep going! 
- Vocabulary is King: Since this is HL, we are looking for "nuance." Don't just say something is "good"; say it is "advantageous," "pivotal," or "transformative." 
- The "So What?" Factor: Always ask yourself why the topic matters in a global context.  

Remember: You aren't being tested on your ability to be a perfect public speaker; you're being tested on your ability to express complex ideas clearly in English.
