Fuente: [Canvas](https://maristasperu.instructure.com/courses/24296/pages/plantilla-english-b-hl-4to). Copia consultada el 26 de octubre de 2026.

In the Language B course, students further develop their ability to communicate in the target language through the study of 

language, themes and texts. In doing so, they also develop conceptual understandings of how language works, 

as appropriate to the level of the course.
