# Session 2- IIT: Students identify situations related to the Impact of Tourism

Pairwork

Title: Postcards from the Mind: A Journey Through Words

ATL Focus:

- Communication: Expressing ideas clearly and creatively in oral and written forms. 
- Social: Collaborating with peers and respecting different perspectives. 
- Thinking: Creative and critical thinking; considering different cultural contexts. 
- Answer the following question : 
- "How does travelling change the way we see the world and ourselves?" 
-  Reflect on personal or imagined travel experiences. 

-  

. Pair Work Writing ACTIVITY :

-  

Write a postcard (150-200 words) from the perspective of someone who has just traveled to a new location. Each postcard must include:

- A cultural observation or interaction. 
- A personal reflection on how the travel experience changed their thinking. 
- A distinct tone or emotional voice (e.g., awe, homesickness, joy, curiosity). 

- Then, exchange postcards. 
- Read the other’s postcard aloud. 
- After reading, express : 

- One open-ended question about the postcard. 
- One positive comment on the writing (style, imagery, insight, etc.).  
- Finally there is a general discussion 
-   

- "What did I learn about communicating culture and experience through writing?" 
- "How did my partner’s feedback help me see my writing differently?"     
-   

   |  

 Watch a VIDEO on The impact of Tourism. Class discussion about the video .

- Have a class discussion about it, they may include other ideas like the impact of Tourism.  

  |  

-  Mention other situations that you have experienced that show impact of tourism is. 
- Write some of the main ideas on the board. 
-   

   |  

- Observe a video, about the negative impacts of Tourism. 
- Afterwards have a class discussion. 
- Write some negative items related to the impact of tourism. 
-  
-  
- Get in teams of three. 
- Set a balance between the positive and negative impacts of tourism . 
- Discuss and present a solution for the negative ones. 
- Present your solutions to the class.  

- Do the listening activity on b. page 63 in pieces of paper. 
- Hand them in to the teacher. 
- Verify answers.
