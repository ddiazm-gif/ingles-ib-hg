# Session 7 IIT: IS LEISURE TIME RECREATIONAL TIME ?

Fuente: [Canvas](https://maristasperu.instructure.com/courses/24296/pages/session-5-iit-is-leisure-time-recreational-time). Copia consultada el 26 de octubre de 2026.

LO : Students describe leisure activities

CAS Short Activity: "The Leisure Swap"

Objective: To get students thinking about how their personal leisure activities can map onto the three strands of CAS, and how they can turn a passive hobby into an active or service-oriented project. Time: 15 minutes

The Setup

Observe that the teacher divides the whiteboard into three columns: Creativity, Activity, and Service.

The Steps

- Write down your absolute favourite leisure activity on a sticky note (e.g., playing video games, baking, scrolling TikTok, playing basketball, reading manga). 
- Come up to the board and stick your note under the column you think it fits best. 

- WHAT'S YOUR CHALLENGE?  IF someone wrote  "playing video games," how can you elevate that to Creativity (streaming/shoutcasting, game design) or Service (organizing a charity gaming tournament)?  
- Get in pairs, pick one standard leisure activity and pitch a 60-second idea on how to turn it into a genuine CAS experiences.
Example: "Reading manga" becomes Creativity by starting a school manga-review blog, or Service by translating short stories for younger language learners.
