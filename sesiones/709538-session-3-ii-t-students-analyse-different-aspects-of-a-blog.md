 PAIR WORK 

- You have to write a SL Personal blog (from 250 - 400 words) 
- Organise your ideas to write a Blog:  

- Your family moved from a rural area to the city when you were a child. And you sat recently with your grandpa who explained to you how the moving has affected the whole family. 
-  

Your task is to explain this migration to your friends and public (group with a general interest) through a blog. Remember it can be a sort of online diary when it is personal.

- Present your work to the teacher using the line paper given per each pair of students.
