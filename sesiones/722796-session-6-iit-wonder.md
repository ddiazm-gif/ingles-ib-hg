Read the book , chapter "THE GRAND TOUR".

   |  

- Get in teams of 4. 
- Each team will represent the chapter according to the teacher's instructions. 
- Consider the tone and the following : 
- Julian’s Weaponized Language: He uses fake politeness in front of teachers, but drops hints and insults when adults leave. 
- Charlotte’s Defensive Politeness: She talks a lot about herself, her acting, and her schedule to avoid dealing with the tension or interacting too deeply with Auggie. 
- Jack's Defensive Aggression: He uses short, sharp corrections ("Shut up, Julian") because he is uncomfortable but wants to protect Auggie. 
- To represent the chapter you may use a rap , a poem , a role-play, a song.   

  |  

READING ASSESSMENT

THE ANALYTICAL MATRIX

 You must prove you understood the linguistic subtext of the chapter by completing this analytical reading grid. Follow the example:

  | CHARACTER  | ACTION FROM THE BOOK  | CODED LANGUAGE

During the tour :

One-word answers like a quiet "Yeah," nodding, or shrugging his shoulders when Julian acts meanly.

After the tour : 

 When reflecting on Julian's behavior, Auggie doesn't tell the adults, "Julian was bullying me" or "He made me feel terrible." Instead, he simply says, "He's not my friend, that's all." Later, when he talks to his mom about Julian, he compares Julian to the character Darth Sidious from Star Wars (the character whose face gets burnt and deformed).

   | THE SUBTEXT (What they actually mean/feel 

  | AUGGIE  |  

 During the Tour:

Auggie constantly looks down, focusing entirely on the other kids' shoes. He notes Jack Will’s gray Adidas, Julian’s green Nikes, and Charlotte’s bright pink Crocs.

After the tour: 

The true emotional climax of this sequence happens right after the tour finishes, when Auggie is back in Mr. Tushman's office and Julian’s mom asks Julian if he was being nice.

And Auggie says he wants to go home.

   |  

During the Tour :

This action is a defense mechanism. By looking at their shoes, Auggie avoids eye contact, which shields him from seeing their shock, pity, or disgust. It also means he doesn't have to show them his own vulnerability. It shows he feels incredibly exposed, anxious, and guarded in this new environment.

After the tour : 

 When Auggie says "He's not my friend," the subtext is a profound sense of disappointment and reality check. He realizes that school will not be an easy, safe haven. By using the Star Wars code with his mom, the subtext is actually a brilliant, witty defense mechanism: he is reclaiming his power. By pointing out that the bad guy in Star Wars looks like him,he proves he is smarter and more self-aware than Julian will ever be.

The Writing Prompt

Choose ONE of the following perspectives: Jack, Julian, or Charlotte. Write a text according to the teacher's instructions. It has to be personal, reflective, detailing your true feelings about leading Auggie on "The Grand Tour."

Your text must explicitly reveal the thoughts you kept hidden during the tour and reflect on your behaviour.

- Length: –150 words. 
- Register: Informal, highly personal, yet reflective.
