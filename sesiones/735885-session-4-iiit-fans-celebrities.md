Criterion A: Language (Productive Skills)

Key Question: How commandingly, fluently, and accurately does the student use spoken English? How varied and appropriate is the vocabulary and grammar?

Berlin Cosmopolitan School

  |  

Grade

   |  

Descriptor

  |  

AD

(Highest Distinction)

   |  

Vocabulary: Command of language is excellent and nuanced. Uses a broad, varied vocabulary, including idiomatic expressions and topic-specific jargon suited to the celebrity's field.

Grammar: Uses a wide variety of complex grammatical structures (e.g., conditionals, passive voice, relative clauses) effectively and purposefully. Minor errors do not hinder communication.

Pronunciation & Delivery: Pronunciation and intonation are natural, clear, and authentic, actively enhancing communication and character portrayal.

  |  

A

(Achieved)

   |  

Vocabulary: Vocabulary is varied and appropriate to the interview context. Attempts idiomatic phrasing with good success.

Grammar: Uses an effective mix of basic and complex grammatical structures. Language is mostly accurate; occasional errors occur in complex structures but do not impede understanding.

Pronunciation & Delivery: Pronunciation and intonation are consistently clear and fluent, with only slight hesitations.

  |  

B

(In Process)

   |  

Vocabulary: Vocabulary is generally adequate and appropriate to the topic, though sometimes simple or repetitive.

Grammar: Relies mainly on basic grammatical structures. Attempts at complex structures contain frequent errors, which sometimes hinder communication.

Pronunciation & Delivery: Pronunciation is clear enough to be understood, but awkward intonation or excessive pauses intermittently disrupt the flow.

  |  

C

(Beginning)

   |  

* Vocabulary: Limited range of vocabulary. Frequently relies on native language translation or repetitive words.

Grammar: Basic structures contain persistent errors. Lack of grammatical variety severely limits expression.

Pronunciation & Delivery: Pronunciation and intonation errors frequently hinder comprehension. Delivery is fragmented or heavily scripted.

Criterion B: Interactive Skills & Message

Key Question: How effectively does the student interact in real time, keep the conversation moving, and develop meaningful ideas/opinions?

Scribd

  |  

Grade

   |  

Descriptor

  |  

AD

(Highest Distinction)

   |  

Interaction & Flow: Demonstrates continuous, authentic engagement. Reacts naturally to non-scripted turns, follow-up questions, and active listening cues with zero artificial pauses.

Depth of Message: Responses and questions show high critical thinking, broad scope, and nuanced opinions. The interview goes beyond biographical facts to discuss inspiration, struggles, values, or cultural impact.

Register: Perfect register alignment throughout (e.g., professional yet warm interviewer vs. articulate, authentic public figure).

  |  

A

(Achieved)

   |  

Interaction & Flow: Maintains a coherent, smooth conversation flow. Responds appropriately to questions and prompt changes with good comprehension.

Depth of Message: Ideas and opinions are presented clearly and developed logically with relevant supporting details or anecdotes.

Register: Register is consistent and appropriate for a formal/semi-formal media interview.

  |  

B

(In Process)

   |  

Interaction & Flow: The conversation flows intermittently. Struggles slightly with unexpected questions or spontaneous turn-taking, relying heavily on a prepared script.

Depth of Message: Answers are simple or superficial. Focuses primarily on basic facts (e.g., birthdate, movie titles) rather than deeper thoughts or experiences.

Register: Occasional lapses in register (e.g., slipping into overly informal slang or breaking character).

  |  

C

(Beginning)

   |  

Interaction & Flow: Interaction is severely limited or awkward. Frequently requires teacher intervention or long pauses to continue speaking.

Depth of Message: Responses are brief, incoherent, or off-topic. Ideas lack development or clear focus.

Register : Inappropriate register for the task context.
