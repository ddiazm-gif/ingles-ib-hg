LO: Students identify Artistic Expressions in an Anglophone country

Here is a short, 15-minute Approaches to Learning (ATL) activity tailored for an English B HL lesson (using the theme of Leisure Time or any current text you are reading).

ATL Activity: "The Critical Lens Swap"

-  

ATL Skill Category: Thinking Skills

-  

Specific ATL Skill: Critical Thinking (Analyzing and evaluating text/media) & Communication (Negotiating meaning) 15 minutes

-  

Read the following article about a social media influencer's post about "hustle culture vs. self-care.

- a social media influencer's post about hustle culture vs. self-care,.docx1.  
- The Setup: Deconstruct the Text 
- Divide into three teams to analyze three lenses 
- Lens 1: The Cultural Anthropologist 🌍 * Question: What does this text tell us about the values, beliefs, or biases of the target audience's culture regarding free time? 
- Lens 2: The Linguist/Stylistic Expert ✍️ 
- Question: How does the specific choice of vocabulary, tone, or register manipulate the reader’s perception of "leisure"? (Look for persuasive devices, metaphors, or loaded words). 
- Lens 3: The Skeptic 🤨 
- Question: What is this text not saying? Whose perspective is missing, and what are the underlying assumptions being made? 
- Each team gathers discuss and present the answer to the question to the whole class. 
- The idea : Together, you must synthesize how looking through multiple angles changes their overall understanding of the text's message. 
- REFLECTION : 
-  

Write a quick exit ticket or share aloud:

"How did explicitly changing your analytical lens change your understanding of the English text compared to just reading it normally? How can you apply this 'lens swapping' when you encounter a difficult text in your Paper 1 or Paper 2 exams?"
