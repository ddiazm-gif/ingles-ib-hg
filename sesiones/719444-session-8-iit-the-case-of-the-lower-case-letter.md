LO : STUDENTS DEVELOP A PRACTISE FOR THE INDIVIDUAL ORAL

 TOK Short Activity: "Is Leisure Universally Understood?"

Objective: To introduce the TOK concept of Perspective and Culture as a shaping factor of knowledge, using text and media consumption as the core focus. Time: 15–20 minutes

The Prompt

Students observe how the teacher writes this central TOK question on the board:

"To what extent is what we consider 'leisure' shaped by our cultural and socio-economic background?"

The Steps

- Observe 1-minute clip of a trending global leisure activity (e.g., the slow-living "ASMR study with me" videos, a traditional sport from another culture).  

Discuss the following questions in small groups:(3)

-  

- How do we know when an activity counts as "productive" versus "leisure"? Who decides that boundary? 
- If a leisure activity requires expensive equipment (like skiing or high-end PC gaming), does that mean certain types of relaxation and personal expression are privileged knowledge/experiences? 
- The Exit Ticket (3 mins): Write down a single sentence answering: “Does your choice of leisure reflect who you truly are, or does it just reflect the society you were raised in?”
