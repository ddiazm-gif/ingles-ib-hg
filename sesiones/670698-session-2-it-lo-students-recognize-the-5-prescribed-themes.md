# Session 2 IT: LO Students recognize the 5 Prescribed Themes

Fuente: [Canvas](https://maristasperu.instructure.com/courses/24296/pages/session-2-it-lo-students-recognize-the-5-prescribed-themes). Copia consultada el 26 de octubre de 2026.

Inquiry activity to introduce students into the 5 Prescribed Themes

The 5 prescribed themes for IB English are typically:

- Identity  
- Experiences 
- Human Ingenuity 
- Social Organisation 
- Sharing the Planet  

CLICK ON THE PICTURE 

STATION BREAKDOWN

Get in teams of 3 and proceed according to the teachers instructions.

 INQUIRY

  |  

REMEMBER.....

- Ss answer some questions getting in pairs: 
- What do you remember about IB English B? 
- What are the five prescribed themes? 
- What skills do we develop in this course?
Give them a minute to jot down ideas first individually and then in pairs.  

   |  

-  Develop a genially activity to reinforce some concepts from the Diploma Programme.

Doing an ACTIVE PAUSE

  |  

Watch the folllowing video related to the 5 Prescribed Themes

   |

- Get in teams of four. 
- Discuss a high-level "Big Question" related to a specific theme.  
- Afterwards share the two questions and answers expressed in your teams. 
- You must use at least two HL-level vocabulary words provided on the slide.

 You have to share which were the most interesting answers , at random.
