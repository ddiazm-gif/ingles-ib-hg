The In-Character Speech (Speaking) Time : 2-3 minutes

The Task: You must "become" the character. This is a Dramatic Monologue or Personal Testimony delivered to the class.

Objective: To express your internal feelings, biases, and emotional growth regarding August Pullman.

Speech Requirements:

- Recall all the information you have mentioned in your RAFT about the character assigned. 
- You must express  feelings like shame, protectiveness, resentment, or awe according to the character assigned.  

Props/Tone: You should adopt the posture and tone of the character .
