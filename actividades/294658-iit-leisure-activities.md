# IIT LEISURE ACTIVITIES

Fuente: [Canvas](https://maristasperu.instructure.com/courses/24296/assignments/294658).

Fecha de entrega: no publicada en la API consultada.

Get in teams of 4 and reflect on the variety of leisure time and recreational activities adolescents enjoy doing in your community.

Divide in categories:

1. The ones that have roots in the past.

2. The ones that have evolved.

3. The ones that can appear in the future according to your perspectives.
